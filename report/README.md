# Field-Study Portfolio Scope

Unlike earlier coursework repositories, this project did not begin from a submitted report.

The portfolio was reconstructed from:

1. direct participation in AI Summit Seoul & EXPO 2026 on 2026.08.20
2. two personal field photos
3. personal recollection of booth demonstrations and conversations
4. official event information
5. official company / product documentation
6. additional manufacturing / quality interpretation created for this portfolio

## Evidence Discipline

### Personal experience
Presented as personal field observation.

### Company facts
Linked to official company / product information.

### Semiconductor manufacturing application
Explicitly labeled as my application idea unless the company itself documents that exact use case.

This prevents the portfolio from overstating the scope of a one-day expo visit.
